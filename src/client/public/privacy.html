Privacy and Terms

Effective October 27, 2026

In short:

  • Content ratings are generated by an AI model. They can be wrong, so check what is important to you.
  • There are no accounts, cookies, ads, or tracking scripts.
  • Your choices, watchlist, and thumbs stay in your browser.
  • If you back up your preferences to Google Drive, your browser talks to Google directly. Neither the web server nor the application backend sees your data.

Privacy

What stays in your browser

Your topic choices, concern limits, streaming services, watchlist, thumbs, "not interested" selections, and search filter are kept in your browser's local storage. The site also downloads the title list and some pages in your browser, so it is fast and works offline. To delete all of it, click "Delete all data" under "Your data" on the Preferences page, or clear the site data for this site in your browser settings.

What the web server and the application backend see

Two parts answer your requests. A web server gets every request and passes it on. The application backend is the Parents' Guide program that answers the request. The source code on GitHub is the application backend and the pages; the web server is set up by the host.

  • Your IP address. Like every website, the web server and the application backend get your IP address with each request. The application backend holds IP addresses in memory for up to one hour, to limit AI lookups, and does not write them to disk. The web server logs your IP address for up to 14 days, to prevent general abuse. See also the EPIXIAN LLC privacy policy.
  • Searches. The application backend sends your search text to TMDB and Open Library to find titles. It keeps the results for up to six hours, so the same search is fast for the next person. The suggestions under the search box are found in your browser as you type: the typed text is sent only when you search. The application backend does not record who searched, and the web server leaves search text out of its logs.
  • Trending titles. To find trending titles, the application backend counts title page views with a one-day anonymous code. It saves only the number of views per title per day, never who viewed them.
  • Done in your browser. Your browser downloads the list of rated titles once and performs most filtering (such as concern limits and streaming services). Recommendations are also generated within your browser using your thumbs and "not interested" signals. Your choices, limits, streaming services, watchlist, and thumbs are never sent to the web server or the application backend.

Other services your browser contacts

Your browser loads posters, covers, and streaming service logos directly from TMDB and Open Library. Those services see your IP address, as with any image on the web. Their own privacy policies apply: TMDB and Internet Archive (Open Library).

Google Drive backup (optional)

On the Preferences page, you can back up your choices to Google Drive and restore them on another device. This is how it works:

  • Nothing connects to Google until you click "Back up to Google Drive" or "Restore from Google Drive".
  • When you click, your browser loads Google's sign-in and asks for one permission: drive.appdata. This lets the site read and write its own hidden app folder in your Drive. It cannot see, read, or change any of your other files.
  • The site saves one file, parents-guide-backup.json, in that folder. It holds the same choices that are in your browser: topics, limits, streaming services, watchlist, thumbs, "Not interested" titles, and search filter.
  • Your browser sends the file directly to Google. Neither the web server nor the application backend receives the file, your Google access token, your name, or your email address.
  • The access token is kept in your browser's memory only, and expires after about one hour.
  • Your browser remembers only that it used the backup, so it can show the "Delete backup" button. It does not store your Google account, name, or email address.

To delete the backup: on the Preferences page, click "Delete backup". This deletes the file and removes the site's access to your Drive. You can also remove the site's access in your Google Account settings, and delete the backup in Google Drive under Settings, Manage apps.

Parents' Guide's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use the data only to back up and restore your choices for you. We do not sell it, share it, use it for ads, or use it to train AI models.

How the ratings are made

Ratings, summaries, themes, and moods are generated by Claude (Anthropic) from public information about each title. The application backend sends only the title's details to Anthropic, never anything about you. Results are saved and shared by all visitors.

Children

The site is a tool for parents and other adults. It does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.
