Privacy and Terms

Effective October 26, 2026

In short:

  • Content ratings are generated by an AI model. They can be wrong, so check what is important to you.
  • There are no accounts, cookies, ads, or tracking scripts.
  • Your choices, watchlist, and thumbs stay in your browser.
  • If you back up your preferences to Google Drive, your browser talks to Google directly, and our server never sees your data.

Privacy

What stays in your browser

Your topic choices, concern limits, streaming services, watchlist, thumbs, "not interested" selections, and search filter are kept in your browser's local storage. The site also downloads the title list and some pages in your browser, so it is fast and works offline. To delete all of it, clear the site data for this site in your browser settings.

What our server sees

  • Your IP address. Like every website, the server gets your IP address with each request. The app holds IP addresses in memory for up to one hour, to limit AI lookups, and does not write them to disk.
  • Hosting. The web server in front of the app can keep its own logs and block abuse. For pg.epixian.com, the host is EPIXIAN LLC: see the EPIXIAN privacy policy for its server logs and abuse blocking. On pg.epixian.com, the web server deletes its logs after 14 days, and leaves search text out of them.
  • Searches. The server sends your search text to TMDB and Open Library to find titles. It keeps the results for up to six hours, so the same search is fast for the next person. The app does not record who searched.
  • Trending titles. To find trending titles, the server counts title page views with a one-day anonymous code. It saves only the number of views per title per day, never who viewed them.
  • Done in your browser. Browse filters titles with your concern limits and streaming services in your browser, and your browser scores the recommendations and "fit" hints. Your choices, limits, and thumbs do not go to the server.

Other services your browser contacts

Your browser loads posters, covers, and streaming service logos directly from TMDB and Open Library. Those services see your IP address, as with any image on the web. Their own privacy policies apply: TMDB and Internet Archive (Open Library).

Google Drive backup (optional)

On the Preferences page, you can back up your choices to Google Drive and restore them on another device. This is how it works:

  • Nothing connects to Google until you click "Back up to Google Drive" or "Restore from Google Drive".
  • When you click, your browser loads Google's sign-in and asks for one permission: drive.appdata. This lets the site read and write its own hidden app folder in your Drive. It cannot see, read, or change any of your other files.
  • The site saves one file, parents-guide-backup.json, in that folder. It holds the same choices that are in your browser: topics, limits, streaming services, watchlist, thumbs, "Not interested" titles, and search filter.
  • Your browser sends the file directly to Google. Our server never receives the file, your Google access token, your name, or your email address.
  • The access token is kept in your browser's memory only, and expires after about one hour.

To delete the backup: in Google Drive, open Settings, then Manage apps. Find Parents' Guide, open Options, and select "Delete hidden app data". To remove the site's access: go to your Google Account connections and remove Parents' Guide.

Parents' Guide's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use the data only to back up and restore your choices for you. We do not sell it, share it, use it for ads, or use it to train AI models.

How the ratings are made

Ratings, summaries, themes, and moods are generated by Claude (Anthropic) from public information about each title. The server sends only the title's details to Anthropic, never anything about you. Results are saved and shared by all visitors.

Children

The site is a tool for parents and other adults. It does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.
